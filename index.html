03 / THE ENGINEER BEHIND THE WORK

Research roots.
Applied engineering.

My first professional work was in aerial imaging and 3D reconstruction. It taught me to look beyond a model’s output and understand the camera, the geometry, and the data behind it.

At the Institute of Space Technology, I worked on multispectral calibration and C++ integration into OpenDroneMap. Learning radiometry, camera models, and photogrammetry while solving a real research problem shaped how I approach unfamiliar work.

Since then, I’ve built client video-automation systems, tracking and segmentation workflows, and multimodal knowledge pipelines. I’ve also taught computer vision and robotics through practical projects on Udemy.

I like the engineering work between a promising model and a useful application: inspecting failure cases, fixing the responsible stage, improving runtime, and making the workflow understandable.
